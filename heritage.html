Centuries of sacred Indian art, revived in living teak.

The sacred temples of South India and the royal havelis of Rajasthan were celebrated for their breathtaking woodwork — carved deity panels, ornate jharokha balconies, and sanctified home shrines that endured for generations. MADHURI FURNITURES continues this venerable discipline, sourcing only ethically harvested, kiln-dried Indian teak and aged rosewood.

Every single feather in our carved peacocks and every ornament on our deity figures is sculpted entirely by hand, using traditional chisels unchanged for centuries. Each piece is hand-oiled with botanical wood finishes that deepen with age — growing more beautiful the longer they live in your home. Custom dimensions and bespoke temple layouts available on request.
